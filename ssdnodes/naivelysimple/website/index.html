Naively Simple builds small, comprehensible tools for publishing, sharing media, and participating in independent online communities.

Explore projects

The idea

Keep the software close.

Your software should work for you, on a machine you control, with data you can reach. We favor direct installation, ordinary files and databases, and interfaces that do not require a platform to function.

Projects

Small pieces, useful together.

Each project has its own scope and release cycle. They share a preference for self-hosting, understandable operation, and user-owned data.
